### CHEM-000544: Pure-water anion-exchange electrolysis with industrial durability
- **Subfield:** Water splitting, hydrogen production and fuel cells
- **Open:** Sustain high-current electrolysis with abundant-metal electrodes and pure-water feed while preserving membrane and ionomer conductivity through aging and interruptions.
- **Status:** A 2026 local-alkalinity design achieved 3 A cm⁻² at 2.08 V and approximately 1,400 hours at 1 A cm⁻². Simultaneous high-current efficiency and service-life durability remain open.
